CONCEPT & LORE

Tank.exe pays homage to early 1990s PC DOS split-screen 3D vehicular combat simulations. Two commanders share a single keyboard to lead opposing tank battalions across a tactical arena.

INITIAL STATUS & FORCES

Each army commands 15 tanks total: 5 active units deployed in line formation on the field and 10 reserves waiting in the hangar. Tanks on both sides display numbers 1 to 5.

Tanks start stationary and accelerate or steer upon your key commands.

ARSENAL & DEFENSE

• Shells (5): High-velocity artillery rounds.
• Mines (2): Deployed behind your tank and arm after one second.
• Smoke (2): Deploys a temporary smoke cloud blocking line-of-sight.

BASE HANGARS

• Rearming: Drive any of your surviving tanks through your home gate into a free hangar slot to fully replenish ammo, mines, and smoke.
• Gate Defense: Hangar slit windows allow defensive surveillance and firing with unlimited ammo to protect against enemy assaults.

VICTORY CONDITIONS

Destroy all 15 enemy tanks to win the battle.
